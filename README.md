## About Me

I am an **Electronics & Communications Engineering graduate from Al-Muthanna University, Iraq**, with interests spanning electronic circuits, industrial control, photovoltaic energy systems, embedded hardware, and communications.

This GitHub profile is an **engineering portfolio**. I prioritize original work, understandable diagrams and documentation, accurate attribution, and clear distinctions between simulation, prototyping, and independently verified hardware.

## Featured Public Work

### [MPPT Controller for Photovoltaic Systems — Graduation Thesis](https://github.com/Durghamrabee/photovoltaic-mppt-thesis)

**Power Electronics · Renewable Energy · PV Systems**

Research and implementation work on **Maximum Power Point Tracking (MPPT)** for photovoltaic systems, including Perturb & Observe (P&O), Incremental Conductance (INC), and adaptive / variable-step approaches with a DC–DC buck converter.
